# General plan

The card type is unclear. Say what you assumed it to be, then cover:

1. **Goal**: what has to be done and why.
2. **Acceptance criteria**: from the checklists and description.
3. **Affected areas**: files, components and data involved.
4. **Steps**: ordered, small changes.
5. **Tests**: what to add or update.
6. **Verify**: how to confirm it works end to end.
7. **Risks**: what could break and how to undo it.
